## Current community promise

Until CoopFT reaches **500 members**, custom bot requests are free under the community promise. Each member may submit one custom bot request every **30 days**. There is no Developer commission during this free period.

After the 500-member milestone is reached and Owner Rank announces the transition, the Developer responsible for a paid custom bot project receives **60% of its confirmed price**. The remaining 40% is retained by the community/service operation.

Do not charge, promise a paid quote, or apply the 60% split before the transition is officially announced.

## Current service limitations

The current deliverable is a tested ZIP containing source code and setup notes. Hosting, deployment, and 24/7 operation are not included. Current payment methods are Robux or Discord Nitro, and payment may be received only by the server Owner or another verified Owner Rank member after a quote is confirmed.
