# Custom Bot Requests

## Intake
Members open a ticket in `#open-a-ticket`, choose **Custom Bot Development**, and complete the **Custom Bot Commission** form. It asks what the bot should do, its main features and workflows, integrations/APIs, database or technical preferences, and expected timeline/support needs.

The private development ticket is visible to the customer, Developer role, Owner Rank, and bot. Ordinary moderators do not have access to commercial project details.

## First review
1. Read every answer.
2. Ask focused questions about missing requirements.
3. Check feasibility and dependencies.
4. Break the request into features and define delivery.
5. Identify permissions, intents, data storage, and security risks.
6. Check the current free/paid policy.

## Before 500 members
Custom bot requests are free under the community promise. Each member may submit one custom bot request every 30 days. Do not request payment or promise a Developer commission during this period.

If demand exceeds capacity, explain the queue and ask Owner Rank how to schedule work. Do not silently break the free promise.

## After the milestone
Only after Owner Rank announces the transition, obtain an approved quote before paid work starts. The responsible Developer receives 60% of the confirmed project price.

## Never
- Start paid work based on an unapproved price.
- Collect payment personally.
- Promise hosting or deployment.
- Ask customers to post credentials in Discord.
- Reuse or disclose another customer's code or data.
