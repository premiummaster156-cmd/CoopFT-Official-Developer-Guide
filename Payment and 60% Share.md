# Payment and 60% Share

## Phase 1 — Before 500 members
CoopFT has promised free custom bot development while the server is below **500 members**.

- Custom bot requests are free.
- One custom bot request per member every **30 days**.
- No Developer commission or customer payment applies during this phase.
- Do not ask for Robux, Discord Nitro, or another payment for a covered request.
- Do not privately bypass the cooldown.

The cooldown is a request limit, not a promise that a project will be completed within 30 days.

## Phase 2 — After 500 members
The paid policy begins only after the server reaches 500 members **and Owner Rank publicly confirms the transition**.

For each approved paid custom bot project:
- Responsible Developer receives **60% of the confirmed project price**.
- Remaining **40%** is retained by the community/service operation.
- Price is based on approved scope and features.
- The split is recorded against the confirmed project price.

Example: on a confirmed price of 100 units, the Developer receives 60 and the community/service operation retains 40.

## Payment handling
Current accepted methods are Robux or Discord Nitro. Payment may be received only by Cookie/Brian (server Owner) or another verified Owner Rank member after the quote and recipient are confirmed.

Developers must never collect payment themselves, request payment to a personal account, or begin paid work based on an informal promise.

## Transition protection
Do not infer activation from an estimate or member-count screenshot alone. Owner Rank must confirm the milestone and effective date. Existing requests accepted under the free policy must be handled according to the transition terms Owner Rank announces.

Only Owner Rank can change the free period, cooldown, payment methods, revenue split, or effective date.
